# Button script for EDEN_AUTOPRESS (scripts/profile-pc.sh): plays a game without a controller so a
# profile can cover gameplay rather than the title screen. Times are seconds since the game started.
#   every <period s> <button> <press ms> from <s> until <s>   - tap a button repeatedly
#   hold <button> from <s> until <s>                          - hold a button (or a d-pad direction)
# Buttons: A B X Y L R ZL ZR PLUS MINUS UP DOWN LEFT RIGHT.
# Menus: A through the title screen and menus.
every 1.5 A 120 from 12 until 50
# Gameplay: move right, jumping now and then.
hold RIGHT from 50 until 600
